# Bcoming Canonical Journey

## The promise

Bcoming helps a person turn what is unique in their life into something useful for people. It supports meaningful work, positive impact, and—where the person wants it—a sustainable livelihood.

It is not a generic business-idea generator. It is a living companion that helps someone recognise the skills, experiences, values, and recurring patterns they can use to contribute.

## The loop

1. The user shares something real: a memory, strength, value, question, action, or difficulty.
2. Atlas makes a useful part visible as a dot, pattern, connection, or timeline moment.
3. Future Self or a mentor connects only the most relevant evidence and offers one next move.
4. The user chooses to act, save it, explore it with a mentor, or keep discovering.
5. Their reflection and real-world learning return to Atlas, making the next guidance more personal.

## The three starting points

- **Discovery:** No clear direction yet. The goal is not to force a project; it is to discover a meaningful direction through personal evidence.
- **Growth:** An idea or early project exists. The goal is to understand the people it serves, try a small version, and learn from feedback.
- **Build:** Something real already exists. The goal is to identify the next valuable milestone and grow it sustainably.

People can begin wherever they are. Discovery can lead to Growth, and Growth can lead to Build, but none of these are rigid labels.

## Project Spark for Discovery

After enough real context, the Council can offer a **Project Spark**, not an automatic project.

A Project Spark includes:

- the exact dots, timeline moments, or reflections it comes from;
- the strength or experience that may be useful;
- the people it could help;
- a small, honest way to explore or test it.

The user always chooses: **Explore this direction**, **Save it for later**, or **Keep discovering**.

## Trust rules

- Evidence before interpretation.
- No invented facts, psychological diagnoses, or absolute identity claims.
- AI-created connections retain their source evidence and can be edited, deleted, or marked unhelpful.
- Mentors receive a small, relevant context pack, never a raw dump of private history.
- Guidance is quiet when evidence is weak; trust is more important than frequency.

## Collective empowerment

Creators is the outward expression of the journey: a place where people building useful, positive work can share progress, support one another, collaborate, and create a better world together.
