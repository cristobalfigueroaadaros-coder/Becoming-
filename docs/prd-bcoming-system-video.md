# PRD: Bcoming System Video

## Purpose
Create a short animated explainer that shows how Bcoming helps a user move from inner chaos to meaningful action.

The first founder video proves that life dots can connect. This second video explains the system that helps the user connect their own dots: Atlas, mentors, projects, and creators.

## Story
1. A symbolic character starts with a noisy mind full of scattered thoughts, ideas, frustrations, and sparks.
2. Bcoming appears as a calm signal.
3. Atlas organizes the chaos into clusters and starts connecting dots.
4. The connected Atlas signals flow into mentor chats, where the idea narrows and becomes clearer.
5. The character receives a mission note/project direction and becomes lighter, inspired, and ready to act.
6. Project work unlocks structure and knowledge.
7. The user connects with creators around the world who are building related things.

## Design Direction
- Comic-like, warm, and symbolic rather than literal or humanoid.
- Use a small luminous traveler/character, mental spark marks, Atlas clusters, mentor cards, mission note, and global creator nodes.
- Match the Bcoming cosmic visual language: dark background, glass surfaces, violet, cyan, gold, emerald, soft glow.
- Keep the animation lightweight with React and Framer Motion for the prototype.

## Duration
- Target: 20 to 24 seconds.
- Avoid long copy. Use short scene labels and motion to explain the system.

## Placement
- First prototype gets a standalone preview route.
- Later placement recommendation: after `Start my map`, before the first Atlas quest, so the user understands why Atlas, Chats, Projects, and Creators are connected.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Shows the full system loop from chaos to Atlas to mentors to project to creators.
- Works responsively on mobile and desktop.
- Does not overlap bottom navigation when embedded in the app.
- Production build passes.
